MACHINE COMPONENT

Steel Bearing

Bearings support moving or rotating components and help reduce friction between contacting machine parts.

Product Overview

Different bearing designs are made to handle different loads, speeds and operating conditions. Selection depends on the shaft, housing and machine application.

Common Applications

  • Electric motors
  • Industrial machinery
  • Conveyors and rotating equipment
  • Pumps and mechanical assemblies

Specifications to Confirm

  • Bearing type and part number
  • Inner and outer diameter
  • Width and load requirements
  • Seals, lubrication and operating speed
