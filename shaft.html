MACHINE COMPONENT

Machine Shaft

A machine shaft is a mechanical component used to support rotating parts and transmit torque or rotational motion.

Product Overview

Shafts are designed according to the forces, speed, dimensions and conditions of the machine in which they are installed.

Common Applications

  • Industrial gearboxes
  • Drive and conveyor systems
  • Rotating machinery
  • Manufacturing equipment

Specifications to Confirm

  • Required length and diameter
  • Material and strength requirements
  • Keyway, threads or other features
  • Tolerances and surface finish
