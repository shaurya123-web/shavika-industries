MACHINE COMPONENT

Industrial Gear

Industrial gears are toothed mechanical components used to transmit rotary motion and torque between machine parts.

Product Overview

Depending on the gear design and arrangement, gears can change rotational speed, torque or direction of motion. The appropriate type depends on the machine's requirements.

Common Applications

  • Industrial gearboxes
  • Manufacturing machinery
  • Material handling equipment
  • Mechanical power transmission systems

Specifications to Confirm

  • Gear type and number of teeth
  • Material and heat treatment
  • Dimensions and bore size
  • Required tolerances and load
